💡 WeatherGPT Overview (Enna ithu?)

WeatherGPT-ngurathu oru Autonomous Multi-Language AI Weather Assistant. Normal weather apps maari verum temperature mattum kaattama, AI Chatbot, Live Doppler Radar Map, Tamil & Tanglish Voice Assistant, Vivasayam (Agriculture), Meenavar (Marine), Aviation, Highway Route Weather, and Downloadable Intelligence Report-nu ellame ore platform-la kudukuthu.

💬 1. Smart AI Chatbot & Tanglish Voice Engine
🧠
Auto Language Detection
Neenga Tamil-la ("ennaiku chennai la mazhai varuma?") illana Tanglish-la ("madurai la rain timing sollunga") type pannalum, language select panna vendiya thevaiye illa! AI thaana purinjikittu athe language-la answer pannum.
🎙️
Two-Way Voice Assistant (Mic & Speaker)
Mic button click panni direct-ah voice-la kelvi kekkalam. AI athukku Indian Accent Tamil/English voice-la full-ah read aloud panni explain pannum. Stop panna stop button-um eruku.
⚡
RAG NWP Real-Time Data
Old data illama, Open-Meteo High-Resolution NWP models (NOAA GFS & ECMWF), WAQI Air Quality, and RainViewer Live Radar-la irunthu live-ah data eduthu answer pannuthu.
🛰️ 2. Live Doppler Radar & Satellite Map
📡
Live RainViewer Doppler Stream
Vaanathula real-time-la mazhai megangal enga irukku, ethana dBZ intensity-la mazhai peyuthu-nu interactive Leaflet Doppler Radar map-la live loop animation-oda paakkalam.
⏯️
Frame Scrubbing & Color Palettes
Play, Pause, Timeline slider moolama past and future rain forecast frames-ah scrub pannalam. Doppler Standard, HD, NOAA NWS color modes-um mathikkalam.
📊 3. Post-Chat Report Generator & Download Confirmation
📑
Automated Intelligence Dossier
Chat-la oru kelvi kettu answer vanthathum, "📊 Full Weather & Live Radar Report-ஐ Download பண்ணவா?" nu card varum.
🛡️
Download Confirmation & Preview Modal
Direct-ah silent download aagama, mudhalla confirmation & preview modal thirakum. Athula live Doppler Radar map, 7-day forecast, AQI, and Agri guidelines-ah paathuttu confirm panni download pannalam.
📥
Multi-Format Export
1. Interactive HTML Document (with embedded live radar map)
2. Print / PDF Export
3. Text Bulletin (.txt)
🌾 4. Sector-Specific Intelligence Tools
🌱
Vivasayam & Crop Advisory
Mann eerappatham (Soil Moisture %), Vidhaippu thaguthi (Sowing status), Marunthu thelippu (Pesticide spraying), and Pasanam (Irrigation) advice.
🌊
Meenavar & Coastal Safety
Kadal alai uyaram (Wave height), kadal seetram, and Meenavargal kadalukku sella pathukaappana nerama nu solra directives.
✈️
Aviation METAR & Visibility
Vimaanam parakka etha VFR/IFR flight category, visibility (km), wind shear, and cloud base details.
🛣️
Highway Route Weather Planner
Oru oorla irunthu innoru oorukku travel pannum pothu (e.g. Chennai to Madurai), valila iruka city-oda weather and rain risk summary.
💍
Event & Wedding Weather Score
Outdoor function, thirumanam, kootangal nadatha mazhai varuma nu 0-100% feasibility score calculate pannum.
🚨
Disaster Emergency SOS
Koduvaana puyal, vellam, idiminnal varumbothu pathukaappu valiuraigal matrum emergency contacts guideline.
