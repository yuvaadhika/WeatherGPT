## 🎬 Overview
This directory contains the **fully automated cinematic real-life simulation** demonstrating the tangible real-world value of WeatherGPT in saving property and protecting citizens through sub-hourly early warning intelligence.

### 🌟 Core Story (55 Seconds Total)
1. **Scene 1: Outdoor Shopping Street (8s)** — A young citizen is outside shopping in a bustling bazaar on a warm, clear day (32°C).
2. **Scene 2: WeatherGPT AI Neural Radar (5.5s)** — WeatherGPT's continuous neural pipeline fuses Doppler satellite radar and ECMWF physics models, predicting a severe convective rain event in ~58 minutes.
3. **Scene 3: Hyperlocal Phone Alert (7s)** — Smartphone vibrates with an exact WeatherGPT alert: *"Rain expected in approx 1 hour. Consider returning home before heavy downpour."*
4. **Scene 4: Person Decides to Return (6s)** — Glancing up at subtle high cloud shifts, the citizen trusts the AI alert and promptly heads home.
5. **Scene 5: Arrives Home & Realization (7s)** — The person arrives safely indoors and suddenly remembers: *"Wait... the clothes drying on the terrace!"*
6. **Scene 6: Clothes on Terrace (8s)** — Strong pre-storm gust front accelerates (34 km/h); the person rapidly collects the laundry from the clothesline.
7. **Scene 7: Heavy Downpour Arrives (8s)** — Exactly on time, torrential monsoon rain hits the terrace floor with splashes and flowing water.
8. **Scene 8: WeatherGPT Success (7s)** — Safe indoors with dry laundry and a warm drink; phone confirms: *"Early warning helped you prepare."*
9. **Final Screen: SIH 2026 Brand & Pillars** — Summary card with *"Replay Simulation ↻"* button.
